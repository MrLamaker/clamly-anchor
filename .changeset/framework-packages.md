First release.

- `@clamly/anchor-react`: `<AnchorText>` (works in Server Components), `<Anchor>` and `useAnchor`.
- `@clamly/anchor-vue`: `<AnchorText>`, the `v-anchor` directive, `useAnchor` and `AnchorPlugin`.
- `@clamly/anchor-svelte`: `<AnchorText>` (Svelte 5), the `use:anchor` action and `anchorSegments`.
- `@clamly/rehype-anchor`: adds anchors to Markdown, MDX and HTML at build time, with no client JavaScript.
